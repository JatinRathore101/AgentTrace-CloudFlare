Setup 1 — Cloudflare account and Free plan

Create the account that hosts AgentTrace. No payment method is needed.

Steps

  1. Sign up at dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up/workers-and-pages using jatin.rathore@skydo.com or a personal email. ✅ confirmed
  2. Verify the email from the confirmation mail.
  3. Stay on the Workers Free plan. Do not add a card. Free covers Workers, Workers AI (10,000 Neurons/day), SQLite Durable Objects and AI Gateway core. ✅ confirmed
  4. Open Workers & Pages once. Choose a workers.dev subdomain (for example jatin-rathore). This sets the final URL https://agenttrace.<subdomain>.workers.dev. 🟡 Wrangler also prompts for it on first deploy
  5. Note the Account ID (dashboard sidebar, or npx wrangler whoami after Setup 2).

⚠️
Do not upgrade by accident

Some Workers AI models require a paid billing method (Kimi, GLM-5.x, DeepSeek v4). Llama 3.3 and Llama 3.1 8B do not. Never select those models in AgentTrace.
