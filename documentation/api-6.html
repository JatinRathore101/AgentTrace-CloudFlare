Base URLs

Local: http://localhost:5173 (vinext/Vite dev default) 🟡. Production: https://agenttrace.<your-subdomain>.workers.dev. The subdomain is chosen in Setup 1 and becomes known after the first deploy. No auth: this is a single-user demo.
